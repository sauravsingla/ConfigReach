Curated 50K benchmark

50,000 controlled cases. Balanced ground truth. One production scanner.

The committed curated benchmark contains 25,000 positive and 25,000 negative scenarios across supported programming languages and configuration formats. Every case has a deterministic expected label, belongs to an explicit version-controlled scenario family, and is scored through configreach.engine.scan.

Claim boundary

Strong regression evidence, clearly scoped.

This is a controlled curated benchmark with explicit, version-controlled scenario families and deterministic ground-truth labels. It is not described as independently human-labelled and is not a claim of universal real-world accuracy.

Positive cases

Real supported configuration accesses and declarations across languages and structured configuration formats.

Adversarial negatives

Comments, inert strings, custom look-alike APIs, dynamic names, quoting traps and structured value-only cases.

Reproducible

The dataset, manifest, aggregate result and row-level predictions are all committed and published.
